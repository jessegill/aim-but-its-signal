# Changelog

## v0.2.0

- Group chats, a conversation-based buddy list with pinning, archiving and drag-and-drop, and
  picture sharing by drag or paste with a description.
- Notifications, sounds and a Dock badge, plus relinking from inside the app when your phone
  removes this computer.
- Fixed messages lost while the app was closed, sends stuck on "sending…", and the QR code
  never appearing.
